Detailed Travel Itinerary

Thursday, Dec 10: Bright Lights Welcome

  • 4:00 PM: Depart Rochester (ROC) on JetBlue B6 2002.
  • 5:37 PM: Arrive at New York (JFK), transfer to hotel, and drop off luggage.
  • Evening: Walk through iconic Times Square to take in the giant digital billboards and festive atmosphere.
  • Dinner: Grab a classic NYC slice or casual burger nearby.

Friday, Dec 11: Financial District, Culture & Broadway

  • Morning: Explore Wall Street, the NYSE, Charging Bull, and the 9/11 Memorial reflection pools.
  • Afternoon: Experience Chinatown and Little Italy. Enjoy authentic pasta or wood-fired pizza for lunch, then walk across the Brooklyn Bridge.
  • Evening: Attend a world-class Broadway Musical (tickets via TKTS or advance group discount).

Saturday, Dec 12: High Line, Parks, Library & Ice Skating

  • Morning: Walk the elevated High Line, browse Bryant Park's holiday markets, and tour the historic New York Public Library (Rose Reading Room).
  • Afternoon: See Grand Central Terminal, the 5th Avenue holiday shop windows, and go ice skating at Rockefeller Center.
  • Evening: Dinner followed by night views from an observation deck (Top of the Rock, The Edge, or Empire State Building).

Sunday, Dec 13: Central Park, History & Flight Home

  • Morning: Explore Central Park (Bethesda Fountain, Bow Bridge) and visit either the American Museum of Natural History or The Met.
  • Afternoon: Board the ferry to Ellis Island and the Statue of Liberty for an educational historical tour.
  • 10:30 PM: Depart JFK on JetBlue B6 2005, arriving back in Rochester at 11:59 PM.
